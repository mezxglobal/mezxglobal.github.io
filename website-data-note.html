This page describes the current static website experience. It is not a substitute for a formal privacy policy for any future product or service.

Contact form

The contact form on this website prepares an email in the visitor's own email application. The website itself does not transmit the form contents to a MezX server or store them in a website database.

External services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts and links to LinkedIn. Those services may process information according to their own policies when a visitor accesses them.

Dashboard figures and interface examples are fictional illustrative data. Do not enter personal, financial, confidential or sensitive information into sample demonstrations.
