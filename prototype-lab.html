MCI / The calm control room

EXPERIMENT 02 / 03
02 — COMPLIANCE INTELLIGENCE

Turn “don't forget” into “all in view”.

A small team has a few recurring obligations. Mark each item as reviewed and watch the progress ring fill. MCI's concept is visibility: what is due, what has evidence, and what still needs a human check.

This is an organizing concept, not legal or tax advice.
