ABOUT MEZX

Business understanding, translated into useful technology.

MezX is an early-stage business and financial technology venture exploring connected approaches to accounting, compliance and financial intelligence.

MezX is at an early stage. This website describes its direction and proposed product concepts; it does not imply a registered corporate status, established customer base, completed certification or generally available software.

What guides the work

Business first

Understand the task, context and people before choosing technology.

Analytical discipline

Use evidence, clear definitions and careful interpretation to reduce ambiguity.

Responsible technology

Design with privacy, traceability, accessibility and human review in mind.

Our product direction

MAI, MCI, MFI and MFS represent four proposed product pillars spanning accounting, compliance, financial intelligence and financial standards. Their scope and technical implementation should be validated through research, prototypes and real user needs.
