MEZX / ARCHITECTURE NOTE

One connected direction. Four focused intelligence layers.

The long-term MezX concept is to explore how specialized business information experiences could share context without losing the clarity of their individual purposes.

Conceptual architecture only. This diagram describes a possible product direction. It is not a representation of deployed infrastructure, live integrations, data pipelines or production systems.
LAYER 01

MAI · Accounting Intelligence

Organize financial records, reporting concepts and accounting context.

LAYER 02

MCI · Compliance Intelligence

Structure obligations, supporting documentation and review activity.

LAYER 03

MFI · Financial Intelligence

Interpret financial and operational indicators in a wider context.

LAYER 04

MFS · Financial Standards

Explore framework references, reporting requirements and jurisdiction-specific rule configuration.

Shared foundation — principles to validate

Information quality

Clear source, period, ownership and definition for important information.

Access & privacy

Purposeful access design and data minimization, to be engineered and tested.

Auditability

Traceable changes, understandable outputs and reviewable assumptions.

Build in evidence-led stages

Discover the problem → validate the workflow → prototype the experience → test reliability → determine whether integration and scale are justified.
